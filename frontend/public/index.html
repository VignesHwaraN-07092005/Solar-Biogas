🛡️ Digester Health Index — Rule-Based Heuristic

Deterministic Composite Operational Stability Score • Bounds: [35, 100]

Mathematical Formulation

Score = max(35, min(100, 100 - P_temp - P_ph - P_press - P_ch4))

The health score starts at a nominal baseline of 100 and applies cumulative deterministic penalties when physical or biochemical variables depart from mesophilic equilibrium.

Parameter Nominal Range Penalty Trigger Penalty

ℹ️ Explicit Methodological Disclaimer

This indicator is a deterministic, rule-based heuristic designed for operator situational awareness and supervisory alerts. It is NOT an ML-trained predictive model and operates independently of the next-day statistical and neural forecasting engines.
